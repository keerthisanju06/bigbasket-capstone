# BigBasket Category Revenue — Data Story

## Overview

This analysis examines delivered-order revenue across six BigBasket categories from January to June 2026. The analysis compares actual revenue with category targets to identify strong-performing categories and categories requiring attention.

## Key Findings

### 1. Household Essentials — Above Target

Household Essentials generated ₹21,715 in delivered revenue against a target of ₹17,000.

- Variance: ₹4,715 above target
- Percentage variance: 27.74%
- Status: Above Target

This is the highest-revenue category and the strongest contributor to overall delivered revenue.

### 2. Personal Care — Above Target

Personal Care generated ₹16,382 against a target of ₹15,500.

- Variance: ₹882 above target
- Percentage variance: 5.69%
- Status: Above Target

Personal Care is performing above target, although its margin above target is relatively small.

### 3. Bakery — Above Target

Bakery generated ₹15,410 against a target of ₹12,000.

- Variance: ₹3,410 above target
- Percentage variance: 28.42%
- Status: Above Target

Bakery shows strong performance and has one of the highest percentage surpluses over target.

### 4. Dairy & Eggs — Watch

Dairy & Eggs generated ₹14,090 against a target of ₹16,500.

- Variance: ₹2,410 below target
- Percentage variance: -14.61%
- Status: Below Target - Watch

The category is close to its target but requires attention to prevent further deterioration.

### 5. Snacks & Beverages — Critical

Snacks & Beverages generated ₹10,895 against a target of ₹13,000.

- Variance: ₹2,105 below target
- Percentage variance: -16.19%
- Status: Below Target - Critical

The category is more than 15% below target and therefore requires corrective action.

### 6. Fruits & Vegetables — Critical

Fruits & Vegetables generated ₹9,790 against a target of ₹12,000.

- Variance: ₹2,210 below target
- Percentage variance: -18.42%
- Status: Below Target - Critical

This is the lowest-revenue category and has the largest percentage shortfall against its target.

## Overall Performance

Total delivered revenue during the six-month period was ₹88,282 from 434 delivered orders.

The average order value was approximately ₹203.41.

Three of the six categories met or exceeded their targets:

- Household Essentials
- Personal Care
- Bakery

Three categories were below target:

- Dairy & Eggs — Watch
- Snacks & Beverages — Critical
- Fruits & Vegetables — Critical

## Recommendations

### Recommendation 1 — Focus corrective action on Fruits & Vegetables

Fruits & Vegetables is ₹2,210 below its ₹12,000 target and has an 18.42% percentage shortfall. Management should investigate demand, pricing, availability and product-level performance in this category and introduce targeted promotions or assortment improvements.

### Recommendation 2 — Improve Snacks & Beverages performance

Snacks & Beverages is ₹2,105 below its ₹13,000 target and is 16.19% below target. The category should be prioritized for promotional campaigns, bundle offers and review of low-performing products to improve delivered revenue.

## Dashboard Interpretation

The dashboard shows that Household Essentials is the strongest category by delivered revenue, while Fruits & Vegetables is the weakest. Monthly delivered revenue reaches its highest point in May before declining in June.

The target-status colors make it easy to distinguish categories that are above target, require monitoring, or require critical corrective action.
